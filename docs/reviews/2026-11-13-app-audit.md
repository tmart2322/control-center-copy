# App audit, 2026-11-13: caps, roadblocks, data, continuity, cost

Five read-only reviewers (Sonnet), one perspective each: Assistant capability, user roadblocks, database, continuity and handoff, cost. Their findings were spot-checked against the code and the live DB before landing here. Placeholders only: no customer names or ids.

## The principle (owner, 2026-11-13)

A cap exists only when one of these forces it:
- **an external system's real limit**, for example Org62's 2,000 rows per query, VDP's 60 calls a minute, or Slack's 1 MiB per canvas;
- **a budget the user sets and can raise**, such as the daily hard cap or the per-message cap;
- **a safety invariant**: nothing external without the user's Apply, Undo on staged changes, connectors read-only.

Anything else is replaced by paging or removed. Claude Code has no per-call caps, and the Assistant shouldn't either.

**Checked:**
- Slack's documented canvas limit is 1 MiB of markdown per `document_content`. The app's cap is 12,000 characters, about 87x smaller.
- Slack documents 300 cells per table, but the canvas created today has a 600-cell table (59 rows × 10 columns), and all 59 rows are in it when read back.
- The 12,000-character figure came from the old autopilot's split size. No measurement behind it was found.

## Why today's hiccups happened (the patterns)

1. **Silent caps under the tools.**
   - Generic reads cut every list to 40 rows. Under budget they step down to 20, 12, 6 and then 3 (`src/server/chat/read.ts` `LEVELS`), with no offset to page.
   - A card holds at most 40 calls (`chat_app_change`) or 20 (`chat_propose_changes`), and a message at most 3 proposals.
   - Per-message limits: 3 drafts, 4 Org62 lookups, 10 VDP changes (`src/llm/chat/tools.ts` `LIMITS`).
   - History and tool-output cuts happen mid-JSON.
   - Canvases are capped at 12,000 characters, which split 20 VDP use cases into 7 canvases.
2. **Computed, never shown.**
   - The DC count lived in a step title the page never draws.
   - `loggingGap` (approved hours that never reached Org62) is computed and never rendered (`src/components/time/derive.ts`).
3. **Two copies that drift.**
   - The DB event bus has its own table→topic map (`src/server/datasource/db/events.ts`) with no entry for `deal_contribution_requests`, `meeting_notes`, `canvases`, `job_schedules` or `channels`. The shared map in `src/server/events/topics.ts` has them.
   - So a claimed DC doesn't refresh Deals ▸ DCs or Log it until a reload.
4. **Preview ≠ Apply.**
   - Only 10 of the 45 stageable routes are checked at staging. The rest fail at Apply, and one failure rolls back the whole card.
   - `version` is stripped from reads, while `patchItem` requires `ifVersion`.
5. **"Done" while work waits.** The Ready stage reads done and Home reads "Nothing missing" while 55 DC requests or 31 use cases wait.

## Findings to fix, by lane

### Lane A: no silent caps (Assistant)
- **A1 (blocker):** reads page instead of cut. `chat_app_read` gets `offset`/`limit` and returns `total`/`nextOffset` as valid JSON; no row cap below the cost cap. Tool-output char cuts become pages too.
- **A2 (blocker):** a card holds any number of calls, displayed grouped (one line per thing), and splits internally if needed. Remove the 40/20 caps. The 3-proposals-a-message guard keys on distinct summaries.
- **A3 (major):** Apply goes per call with a receipt of what failed (continue on error), instead of rolling back 54 good claims for 1 bad one. Undo stays per change set.
- **A4 (major):** keep `version` in reads, and auto-fill `ifVersion` at Apply when the model omits it.
- **A5 (major):** remove the per-message count limits (drafts, lookups, VDP, research, proposals). The cost cap is the only bound, and launches stay user-confirmed.
- **A6 (major):**
  - History keeps user messages whole (up to the model's context).
  - Assistant replies are kept to 8,000 characters, and the proposal lines are carried.
  - `maxTurns` clamp 40 → 80.
  - Drive pages 12k → 60k.
  - Gmail bodies uncut, or paged.
- **A7 (major):** loosen the routes that need no denial:
  - `getBrief`, `previewPolicy` and `searchOrg62Opps` become read;
  - `markItemSeen`, `undoAudit`, `retryJob`, `checkMissingForecast` and `syncForecast` become stage.
  The real deny list stays: sends and posts, Apply, credentials, dry run, scan and agent launches, raising its own budget, dev routes.
- **A8 (minor):**
  - SOQL LIMIT 200 → 2,000 (Org62's own per-query maximum), and the IN list to match.
  - Add Contact and OpportunityContactRole to the read-only object list.
- **A9 (minor):**
  - Drop the duplicate `chat_read` catalog and the bespoke reads that map one-to-one to routes (kept as aliases). This makes the per-turn prefix smaller, and cache writes are 69% of chat cost.
  - A "available, not active for your role" line goes in the Persona block.
  - Complete the receipt entity map.

### Lane B: logging canvases (one canvas, a full review, never twice)
- **B1 (blocker):** canvas cap 12k → close to Slack's 1 MiB (with a margin).
  - One canvas per batch. Use cases ride on it, as D-67 intended (never canvases of their own).
  - Use cases per batch: all of them.
  - Use-case text is cut at the Org62 field's real length (read from the schema), not 1,200.
- **B2 (major, owner request):** a real review before Apply.
  - Every row that will be written, in readable tables by kind: Events, DCs, use cases and updates.
  - Each table has its count and the fields Slackbot sets, plus search.
  - **Remove a row** before Apply (it goes back to waiting).
  - Today the panel shows raw markdown in a 320 px box.
- **B3 (major):** a crash or restart mid-send can re-run a live write.
  - Today `outbox.dispatch` gets 5 attempts, and the lease reaper requeues a job whose draft is still `sending`.
  - Fix: `maxAttempts: 1`, the reaper never requeues `outbox.*`, a durable "write started" mark, and on re-entry a read-back instead of a second write.
  - A canvas relay timeout looks for the canvas before offering Retry.
- **B4 (minor):** a generic proposal applies with its stored `base_version`, so an old card can't overwrite newer data.

### Lane C: nothing hides (user surfaces)
- **C1 (blocker):** one table→topic map, used by the DB bus too, plus a test that every audited table maps to a topic. Claims, canvases and meeting notes then refresh live.
- **C2 (major):** waiting DCs and use cases count everywhere work is counted: the sidebar Time badge, Home ▸ Log & fix, and a Ready stage that isn't "done".
- **C3 (major):** render the logging-gap warning ("N h approved, not in Org62, last seen DATE").
- **C4 (major):** truncated names and reasons get a tooltip. A DC row's lock reason gets its own line, not last in a cut sentence.
- **C5 (major):** error toasts stay until dismissed. `ApiError` strips HTML and never cuts mid-sentence. The VDP push result lists every failure.
- **C6 (major):** long lists get "Show N more" or virtualization (pipeline, Needs you, DC groups). Bulk fixes run as a batch with progress. "Withdraw all waiting" DCs.
- **C7 (minor):**
  - "See all" on Since.
  - A searchable task picker in Start in Claude (it's capped at 40 today).
  - An Assistant badge for unapplied cards.
  - Capture's toast says where the item landed.
  - Ready doesn't flash "Nothing ready" while counts load.
- **C8 (guardrails):**
  - A script that fails on exported derive/model fields no component reads.
  - A table-driven test: "a pending count above 0 never yields a done or empty state".

### Lane D: cost (measure first)
14-day spend (really 4.6 days, since the Nov 9 rescan): **$71.67**, about $10–13 a day steady.

| Skill | $ | Share |
|---|---|---|
| Slack scan | 20.33 | 28% |
| Claude sessions | 12.09 | 17% |
| read-ins | 9.39 | 13% |
| settle | 7.58 | 11% |
| chat | 6.95 | 10% |

- **D1:** record cache read/write tokens on every call. Scanner rows have none, and 39% of dollars sit on rows with 0 tokens, so savings can't be measured yet.
- **D2:** automatic read-ins on Haiku (Sonnet only when asked), and skipped when the evidence hasn't changed. They hit their $2/day line every day; this saves about $20–40 a month per user.
- **D3:**
  - The Claude-sessions scanner doesn't call the model on a busy ↔ idle flip, and runs at most once per 5 min per session.
  - 1,026 of 1,275 incremental runs saw 1 item. This saves about $15–40 a month.
- **D4:** settle records whether a Sonnet escalation changed the verdict. It escalates 38% of items at 10x the price; drop the escalation where it doesn't change the outcome.
- **D5:** Slack no-op pre-check with a cheap count before a full traversal (49% of runs found nothing).
- **D6:** reserve the cap before a call, not check-then-spend. Add a budget guard on the three relay jobs. The first-scan default goes $100 → $25, with an estimate shown.

### Lane E: teammate-safe updates and data
- **E1 (major):** `npm run update` changes:
  - backs up before migrating;
  - builds before migrating, so a failed build leaves the schema alone;
  - asks before a major release's migration;
  - stops both services while migrating.
  The worker also refuses to claim jobs when its bundled migration and the DB disagree.
- **E2 (major):** backups. Doctor warns when the last backup is over 48 h old, plus an optional copy folder. The README says what is and isn't recoverable.
- **E3 (major):** VDP sign-in.
  - Ship the cookie-refresh script, or document where it comes from, along with the PAT and the 1-hour lifetime.
  - Persist the auth pause so a restart doesn't retry every cycle.
- **E4 (major):** retention for `jobs`, `scan_runs` (which cascades to ledger and events), `llm_calls` (after rolling up), and health checks. Decide an audit horizon. The DB is 255 MB after one week.
- **E5 (minor):**
  - A test that migration files, journal and timestamps agree. Today the numbers skip 0018, 0019 and 0021, and an older timestamp merged later would be skipped silently.
  - Classify "Streamable HTTP error" as a gateway outage.
  - Doctor checks the Node path baked into the LaunchAgents.
- **Backlog, not now:**
  - move the `cc.ext/<table>/<id>` side-car (3,819 kv rows) into real columns;
  - owner-prefix the global idempotency indexes (only matters if users ever share a DB);
  - set-null instead of cascade on user-authored rows (no app path deletes opps today).

## Fine as is (don't touch)
- **Safety invariants:**
  - the SELECT-only SOQL guard and object allowlist (widened as above);
  - the `patchSettings` guard on sending and dry run;
  - persona hiding, which doesn't remove anything.
- **DC staging:** preview-first DC logic, which matches Apply.
- **Drive:** reads page with an offset.
- **Job queue:** leases, dedupe and dead-letter; no stuck or dead jobs live.
- **Double-click Apply guards:** status, `ifVersion` and idempotency key.
- **Chat plans:** they detect "changed since it was proposed".
- **Audit trigger:** the trigger and its 90-day scrub.
- **Model spend attribution:** fixed and accurate.
- **Live state:** no stuck sends or runs.
